Agenda

We post reading groups, swaps and workshops to radar.squat.net, the shared calendar used by squats, infoshops and radical community spaces. The list below is our group's events pulled straight from Radar, so it's always up to date — add or edit events on Radar itself, not here.

Setup note for whoever maintains this page: replace YOUR_GROUP_ID in the iframe URL below with your Radar group ID (find it on your group's Radar page — the number at the end of the URL). If you'd rather not use an iframe, Radar also offers a small JavaScript embed widget and a plain results-list URL you can just link to instead — see their embedding guide for both options.

Our reading corner, open every Saturday

est. by volunteers, run by community

Books by, for, and about women, trans and non‑binary lives.

At The Feminist Library you can read and borrow books on feminism and movements for liberation for a small deposit. We are an anti-imperialist, anticolonial, antiracist feminist library and the collection includes non/fiction, theoretical and practical books, children’s books and zines on topics such as liberation movements, black and queer activism, indigenous knowledges, feminist marxism, lesbian and trans histories, abolitionism, ecology, embodiment and health, and feminist art. The Feminist Library approaches feminism as the project for the liberation of all. Everyone is welcome. As a collection of books, the library is and will always be incomplete. Currently, our collection only includes books in Dutch and English.

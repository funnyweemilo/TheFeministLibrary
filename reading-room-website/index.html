From the shelves — feminist theory & fiction

Zines, memoirs & well-loved paperbacks

Our reading corner, open every Saturday

est. by volunteers, run by community

Books by, for, and about women, trans and non‑binary lives.

The Reading Room is a small, volunteer-run feminist library. We collect fiction, theory, zines and memoir that mainstream libraries overlook, and lend them out for free. No membership fees — just bring the book back so the next person can read it too.

the card catalog

Full catalog

Search and browse everything we hold, powered by TinyCat. Anyone can search and see what's available here — no login needed. Borrowing history and patron details stay private to our volunteer team, who manage checkouts on the admin side. Looking for something we don't have? Try WorldCat or Open Library to see who else holds it.

Setup note for whoever maintains this page: replace YOUR_LIBRARY_ID in the iframe src below with your TinyCat library ID (the part after /lib/ in your TinyCat homepage URL, e.g. librarycat.org/lib/YOUR_LIBRARY_ID). Everything else — adding books, checking items in/out, editing records — happens on TinyCat itself; nothing here needs updating when the catalog changes.
